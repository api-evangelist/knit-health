# Knit Health

> Knit Health Technologies, Inc. is a UC Berkeley spin-out building a Large Clinical
> Behavior Model (LCBM) — a healthcare-native AI trained on the real decision patterns
> of practicing clinicians (referrals, scheduling, routing, discharge timing, care
> coordination) captured in EMR data, rather than on medical text alone. It is sold to
> health systems as an enterprise intelligence platform. Knit publishes no public API,
> developer portal, or SDK; integrations are delivered as bespoke APIs designed with
> customer engineering teams.

## Use cases

- [Intelligent Specialist Routing](https://www.knit.health/use-cases/intelligent-specialist-routing): Model care pathways to accelerate or redirect patients.
- [Predictive Patient Flow](https://www.knit.health/use-cases/predictive-patient-flow): Real-time patient journey analysis to unblock hospital flow.
- [Optimized Care Team Allocation](https://www.knit.health/use-cases/optimized-care-team-allocation): Match ambulatory patients across MDs, APPs, and virtual care.
- [Actionable Clinical Variation Insight](https://www.knit.health/use-cases/actionable-clinical-variation-insight): Learn from top-performing clinicians to find systemwide improvements.
- [Proactive Care Recommendations](https://www.knit.health/use-cases/proactive-care-recommendations): Monitor records to surface emerging care needs.
